Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: November 7, 2026

In short: Cabin Deck does not collect personal data. It has no servers, no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share data. Whatever the app stores stays on your device. When you use a feature that needs an outside service (a radio stream, song recognition), your device contacts that service directly, never through us.

Scope

This policy covers the Cabin Deck app on every device it runs on (iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Mac and CarPlay), the in-car web player at nadav-lab.github.io/cabindeck/tesla, and this website. It is written to meet Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, as amended by Amendment 13 (in force since August 14, 2025), and, where they apply, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California privacy law (CCPA/CPRA).

Who is responsible (controller)

The developer of Cabin Deck, as listed as the seller on the app's App Store page ("we"). Contact for any privacy matter, including requests under the law: nadav955@gmail.com. We maintain no database of personal data, so no Data Protection Officer is required.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not receive, see or keep any data about you or your use of the app: no name, email, location, device identifier, listening history or usage data. The app's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". If you email us, we use what you send only to reply, and delete it on request.

What is stored on your device only

Recognized songs; favorite songs and stations; stations you added; podcast subscriptions, playback positions and the order of the podcasts page; listening statistics for the monthly summary (time per station per month); audio and video files and links you added, and access to folders you linked (the app reads only the folder you chose, to list and play its files); settings, including the alarm and sleep timer; and, if you connect Spotify or enter an AudD key, the Spotify access token (in the encrypted device Keychain) and the AudD key. This data is protected by the operating system's encryption, is never sent to us, and is kept until you delete it: song history with "Clear", files and folders by swiping, and everything by deleting the app. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may back it up under its own settings and policy.

Services your device contacts directly

Only when you use the matching feature, your device connects directly to the services below. We receive no copy. Each sees your IP address and device type as part of normal internet traffic, and acts under its own privacy policy:

  • Radio stations and their streaming servers (including Galgalatz and Galei Tzahal, Kan, 99FM, 103FM, Radio Tel Aviv, Radius, Galei Israel, Radio Haifa, Radio HaTachana, A-Shams and Reichman University's Kol HaUniversita): live audio. Now-playing data is also read from the Kan and Galgalatz websites.
  • Apple: ShazamKit recognition (a digital signature of a short audio sample, not the audio itself); adding songs to your Shazam library, if enabled; Apple Music, if connected, including creating playlists in your library when you ask; the podcast directory and album artwork; Siri and voice search; and alarms (AlarmKit), managed by the operating system.
  • Deezer: song title and artist only, for artwork and a song link.
  • Spotify, if connected: to read what is playing on your account.
  • AudD, only if you entered your own AudD key: an audio clip of about 10 seconds, for recognition only.
  • Podcast publishers and their hosts (such as Omny, NPR and the BBC): episode audio.
  • Addresses you add yourself ("Add from link"), such as a Google Drive file or an RSS feed: the app contacts only the address you entered.
  • Apps and sites you choose to open or share to: "Lyrics" opens a Google search in your browser; song links open in Apple Music, Spotify, Deezer or YouTube; sharing to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or another app passes only the image and text you chose to share, and only when you choose to. From then on, that service's privacy policy applies.

Some of these services operate outside Israel, so contacting them transfers your IP address abroad, at your initiative, as part of the feature you asked for.

Device permissions

Microphone: only when you tap "identify from microphone" or voice search, for a few seconds; the recording is not stored. Speech recognition and Siri: for voice commands and search, processed by Apple. Apple Music and media library: to show and play your playlists, create playlists when you ask, and read the song playing in the Music app. Photos (add only): to save a share card you created; the app does not read your photos. Alarms: so the alarm rings at the time you set. Files and folders: only those you explicitly pick. You can revoke any permission in device Settings at any time.

The in-car web player and this website

Both are hosted on GitHub Pages, operated by GitHub, which may log IP addresses and technical data under its own privacy policy. The in-car player keeps your favorite stations and last station only in your browser's local storage, loads a playback component (hls.js) from Cloudflare's cdnjs, and both load fonts from Google Fonts. Streams and Kan's now-playing data come directly from the stations. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Legal basis

We do not process personal data, so we rely on no legal basis for processing. Processing your device performs locally, and requests to outside services, happen at your request to provide the feature you chose, subject to the permissions you granted.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share personal data (including "selling" or "sharing" as defined under California law), show ads, use analytics or tracking, build profiles, make automated decisions about you, or track you across other companies' apps and websites. The app is completely free.

Your rights

Under Israeli law you may access personal data held about you and ask to correct or delete inaccurate data. Under the GDPR, where it applies, you also have the rights to erasure, restriction, portability, objection and withdrawal of consent. Under California law you have the rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete; local data is deleted in the app or by deleting it. For data held by outside services, contact them. You can send any request to us and we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to Israel's Privacy Protection Authority or to the data protection authority in your country.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 (as defined by the US COPPA) or under 16.

Security

Having no servers, we hold no database that could be breached. Data on your device is protected by the operating system's encryption and security, tokens are kept in the Keychain, and connections to outside services are mostly encrypted (HTTPS).
