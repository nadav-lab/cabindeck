Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

In short: Cabin Deck does not collect personal data. It has no servers, no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Whatever the app stores stays on your device.

Who is responsible

The app is developed and operated by an individual developer ("we"). Privacy questions: nadav955@gmail.com.

What is stored, and where

Your recognized-song history, favorite stations and songs, podcast subscriptions and playback positions, and settings are stored only on your device. If you connect Spotify, its access token is stored in the device Keychain. None of this is sent to us; we run no server that receives it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Services your device contacts directly

To work, your device connects directly to the services below. We do not receive a copy of what is sent. Each service acts under its own privacy policy and sees your IP address as part of normal internet traffic: radio stations and their streaming servers (live audio), and the Kan and Galgalatz websites (now-playing data and the weekly playlist); Apple (ShazamKit recognition, which sends a digital signature of a short audio sample rather than the audio itself; the podcast directory and album artwork; Apple Music if connected; Siri and voice search); Spotify, if connected, to read what is playing on your account; LRCLIB and Deezer, which receive only the song title and artist, for lyrics, artwork and song links; AudD, only if you entered your own AudD key, which receives an audio clip of about 10 seconds for recognition only; and podcast publishers, whose servers deliver the episodes. Some of these services operate outside Israel, so contacting them transfers your IP address abroad.

Device permissions

Microphone: only when you tap "identify from microphone", for a few seconds, to recognize a song; the recording is not stored. Apple Music and media library: to show and play your playlists and to read the song playing in the Music app. Siri and speech recognition: for voice commands and voice search, processed by Apple. You can revoke any permission in device Settings at any time.

What we don't do

We do not sell, rent or share personal data, show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or build user profiles. The app is completely free, with no payments or purchases.

Your rights

Under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 1981, and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may know what personal data is held about you, access it, correct it and request its deletion. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access or erase; deleting the app erases the local data. For data held by the third-party services, contact them directly. If you believe your privacy has been infringed, you may contact us, Israel's Privacy Protection Authority, or the data protection authority in your country.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
